01

Overview

Cue is a notification scheduling app. It intercepts notifications on your device, holds the ones you've scheduled for later, and delivers them at the time you choose. None of this requires an internet connection, a user account, or any communication with our servers — because we don't have any.

Cue is a local-first application. Every piece of data the app handles — notification titles, app names, timestamps — is stored in a private database on your own device and never transmitted anywhere.

02

What we access and why

To do its job, Cue needs to read certain information from your phone. Here is exactly what we access and why:

Notification titles and short text

When Cue holds a notification for later delivery, it stores the notification title and a short text snippet so it can show you a meaningful summary in your daily catch-up. We read only what Android exposes at the notification surface level — we never read the full content of messages, emails, or chats.

App names and package identifiers

Cue reads the names of apps installed on your phone to build your scheduling list. This information never leaves your device.

03

How your data is stored

All data is stored in a private, sandboxed database on your device using Android's Room database system. No other app can access this database.

🗑️ Auto-deletion: Cue automatically deletes all notification data older than 7 days. You can also clear everything manually at any time from the app settings.

☁️ No backups: Cue data is excluded from Android cloud backups. Your notification history never leaves your device, even through backup systems.

04

Third-party sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties. We are not able to — we never receive your data in the first place.

Cue contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics libraries, and no tracking code of any kind.

05

Permissions

Cue requests the following Android permissions:

🔔
Notification Access Required Allows Cue to intercept, hold, and schedule notifications. This is the core function of the app. Granted once via Android's Notification Access settings.
⏰
Exact Alarm Required Allows Cue to deliver your daily notification digest at the exact time you choose.
📊
Usage Stats Optional Used for the Sync-on-Read feature — automatically clearing an app's held notifications when you open that app directly. The app works fully without this permission.
📱
Foreground Service Required Keeps Cue's background service running reliably. Android requires this for any app that monitors notifications continuously.
🔄
Receive Boot Completed Required Restarts Cue's service automatically when your phone reboots, so scheduling continues without you having to reopen the app.

06

Children's privacy

Cue is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

07
